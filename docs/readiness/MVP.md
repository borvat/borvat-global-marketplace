# MVP launch boundary

Proposed launch essentials: verified buyer identity/profile; search/categories/filters; product with seller-offer comparison; real seller KYB and independent review; private store/offer/order operations; server-authoritative one-seller checkout, stock reservations and PSP-supported sandbox-then-separately-approved-live flow; tracking/fulfilment; statutory withdrawal and returns/refund; versioned commission and provider/Accounting reconciliation; support/reviews/moderation; tenant isolation, transactional audit and operational restore/monitoring.

Later: unified multi-seller payment, cross-border markets/currencies, ads/campaigns/coupons, repricing, auctions, loyalty/wallets, native mobile and advanced fraud models. Necessary consumer/privacy/safety obligations cannot be deferred as advanced features.

No current launch approval. See original EU/payment study and its conditional scope decisions; final legal entity, seller contracts, DSA/DAC7 classification, PSP underwriting and tax treatment unresolved. No demo financial/shipping metric is production evidence.
